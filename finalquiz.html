🏆 The Final Exam 🏆

You've learned so much! Let's put all your skills to the ultimate test.

1. Which sentence contains a Proper Noun (खास नाम)?
2. What is the correct way to write this sentence?
3. Find the Action (Verb) in this sentence: "The teacher reads a book."
4. Find the Describing Word (Adjective): "She is wearing a beautiful dress."
5. Replace the noun with a Pronoun: "___ is a good boy."
6. Find the Adverb (how the action is done): "The turtle walks slowly."
7. Choose the sentence with the correct order (Who + Action + What).
8. Fill in the Preposition: "The bird flew ___ the tree."
9. Choose the correct Conjunction: "I want to play outside, ___ it is raining."
10. Fill in the Interjection: "___! We won the match!"
11. Which sentence is happening in the Past Tense (Yesterday / कल)?
12. Complete the sentence for the Future Tense: "Tomorrow, I ___ to school."
13. Which word shows the action is happening RIGHT NOW (Present)?
14. Choose the correct Determiner: "___ apple is red."
15. 🌟 MASTER CHALLENGE 🌟

Identify the Adjective AND the Verb in this sentence:
"The fast cheetah runs."
