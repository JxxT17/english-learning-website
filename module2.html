Module 2: Building Sentences (वाक्य बनाना)

Let's put the words together like puzzle pieces!

Step 1: The Magic Rule ✨

English sentences usually follow a simple rule:

Who (कौन) + Action (काम)

He (वह) + runs (दौड़ता है).
= He runs. (वह दौड़ता है।)

Step 2: Adding a Thing 🍎

Now let's add a Noun at the end to say what is happening.

Who (कौन) + Action (काम) + What (क्या)
  • 👦 Boy (Who) + eats (Action) + 🍎 apple (What).
    The boy eats an apple. (लड़का सेब खाता है।)
  • 👧 She (Who) + 🎾 plays (Action) + tennis (What).
    She plays tennis. (वह टेनिस खेलती है।)

Step 3: Making it Beautiful! 🎨

Let's put an Adjective (विशेषण) before the thing to describe it.

Who + Action + Describing Word + What

👦 The boy + eats + a red + 🍎 apple.
= The boy eats a red apple.
