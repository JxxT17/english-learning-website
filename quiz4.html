Quiz 4: Time Travel (Tenses)

Let's see if you can match the verbs to the right time!

1. Which sentence is in the Past Tense (भूतकाल - बीता हुआ समय)?
2. What is the magic word we use for the Future Tense (भविष्य काल)?
3. Which word shows the Present Tense (वर्तमान काल - अभी का समय)?
4. Fill in the blank: "Yesterday, I ___ food." (कल मैंने खाना ___)
5. Fill in the blank: "Tomorrow, he ___." (कल वह ___)
