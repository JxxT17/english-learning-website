Basic Tenses (काल)

1. Past Tense (भूतकाल - बीता हुआ समय) 🔙

When an action has already happened (Yesterday / कल).

Hint: Many past verbs end with -ed.
  • I played. (मैंने खेला।)
  • He jumped. (वह कूदा।)

2. Present Tense (वर्तमान काल - अभी का समय) 🔽

When an action is happening right now or happens every day (Today / आज).

Hint: Simple verbs, sometimes ending in -s for he/she.
  • I play. (मैं खेलता हूँ।)
  • He jumps. (वह कूदता है।)

3. Future Tense (भविष्य काल - आने वाला समय) 🔜

When an action will happen later (Tomorrow / आने वाला कल).

Hint: Always uses the magic word will before the verb!
  • I will play. (मैं खेलूँगा।)
  • He will jump. (वह कूदेगा।)

Quick Review Table 📝
